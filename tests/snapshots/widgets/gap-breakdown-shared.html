Look-ahead leakage = rows used before their available_at. Data issues = wrong or revised values (restatements, survivorship, bad rows). A row is counted in one of the two, never both.

Bar length is the part’s share of the gap. The parts add up to the gap exactly; what cannot be attributed to a named cause is shown as Unexplained and labelled UNKNOWN, not explained away. Shared view: only each part’s share of the gap is shown; the values and the total are withheld.
