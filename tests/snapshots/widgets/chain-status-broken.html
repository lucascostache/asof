What this checked

  • Every event’s payload matches its recorded hash.
  • Each event links to the hash of the one before it, in order, with no gap.
  • The latest event matches the tail anchor kept beside the ledger (so a cut-off end is seen).

What it does not show

  • That a source’s timestamps are true: only that they are unchanged since they were written.
  • That the anchor itself is trustworthy: copy the head to somewhere you trust and verify against that.
